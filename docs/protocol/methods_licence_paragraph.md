## AlphaGenome licence and compliance

AlphaGenome model parameters and output were accessed under the AlphaGenome Model Parameters Terms of Use (https://storage.googleapis.com/alphagenome/terms/AlphaGenome-Model-Terms-of-Use.pdf) and the AlphaGenome Output Terms of Use (https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/output-terms). The corresponding author is affiliated with Barts Health NHS Trust, a non-commercial organisation within the Terms' definition, and this work was carried out in an independent-research capacity with no commercial funding, no research on behalf of a commercial organisation, and no downstream commercial use of AlphaGenome outputs or derivatives.

Prior to any predictor query the study protocol, variant list, and analysis plan were pre-registered on the Open Science Framework (osf.io/5hvgf; registration DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/6PGX8, 2026-09-13). A courtesy notification was sent to the AlphaGenome team on 2026-09-14 describing the pre-registered analysis; the AlphaGenome team replied on 2026-09-15 that they do not provide legal advice and recommended independent legal review. The study proceeds under the researchers' non-commercial-academic-affiliation reading of the two Terms documents, noting that neither document contains any clause restricting benchmarking, evaluation, comparison to other splice-prediction models, or publication of quantitative discrimination or rank-correlation metrics computed on AlphaGenome output.

No machine learning model was trained, fitted, or calibrated on AlphaGenome output within this study. Analyses computed on AlphaGenome output are restricted to receiver operating characteristic area under the curve, paired DeLong confidence intervals on ROC-AUC differences, Spearman rank correlation with 10,000-iteration bootstrap confidence intervals, and descriptive expressivity tallies. Per-variant AlphaGenome scores and all derived statistics are released in the supplementary data alongside a conspicuous notice, following the AlphaGenome Model Parameters Terms of Use Section 3(c) and Output Terms of Use Section 3, that these outputs and derivatives are provided under and subject to the AlphaGenome Output Terms of Use (https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/output-terms).

The pre-registered banned-word list is applied to the full manuscript; no prescribing, patient-selection, treatment-eligibility, procurement, commissioning, or clinical-decision-support claim is made. The following disclaimer, quoted verbatim from Section 8 of the AlphaGenome Model Parameters Terms of Use, applies to all AlphaGenome-derived content in this manuscript and its supplementary materials:

> AlphaGenome Assets are for theoretical modeling only. They are not intended, validated, or approved for clinical use. You should not use AlphaGenome Assets for clinical purposes or rely on them for medical or other professional advice. Any content regarding those topics is provided for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional.
